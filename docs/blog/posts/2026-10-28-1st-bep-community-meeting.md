# Thank you!

Thank you all for a great 1st BEP community meeting! We had a total of 12 BEPs presenting their progress, questions and issues. We believe that these meetings are a great opportunity to accelerate the BEP process and learn from each other. We will have one meeting in each quarter and depending on the progress of each BEP we will select the presenters for the next meeting. This is mostly because we realized that we would like to have more time at the end for issue discussion and resolution.
